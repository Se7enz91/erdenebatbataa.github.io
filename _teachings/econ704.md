## Хичээлд ашиглах судалгааны ажлууд

1. Cornwell, C. & Rupert, P., 1988. [Efficient Estimation with Panel Data: An Empirical Comparison of Instrumental Variable Estimators](http://www.josefajardo.com/teaching/CR88.pdf). *Journal of Applied Econometrics* 3, 149-155

2. Riphahn, R.T., Wambach, A. & Million, A. 2003. [Incentive Effects in the Demand for Health Care: A Bivariate Panel Count Data Estimation](http://people.stern.nyu.edu/wgreene/Lugano2013/RWM-MixedModel.pdf). *Journal of Applied Econometrics* 18(4), 387-405

3. Gregory, C.A. & Deb, P. 2015. [Does SNAP improve your health?](http://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~wgreene/Lugano2013/SNAP-Bivariate-OP-Probit.pdf) *Food Policy* 50, 11-19

4. Б. Эрдэнэбат. 2020. [Монгол эдийн засагчид болон эдийн засгийн байгууллагуудын чансаа](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/345718306_Mongol_edijn_zasagcid_bolon_edijn_zasgijn_bajguullaguudyn_cansaa_Ranking_of_Mongolian_economists_and_economic_institutions). *Эдийн засаг: Онол, практик* 34, 2-35
